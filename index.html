Center stays put

The averages pile up around the population mean μ, for every sample size. Sample means are unbiased.

Spread shrinks like 1/√n

The SD of the averages is σ/√n. Quadruple n and the bottom chart gets half as wide.

Shape turns normal

Even for skewed, U-shaped or two-humped populations, the averages look close to normal once n is moderately large. Try the coin with n = 2, then n = 30.
