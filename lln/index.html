The gap between the count and n·p keeps wandering, and tends to grow, even while the proportion settles. Number of flips on a log scale.

Proportions settle

After a few flips the share of heads can be far from p. After thousands, it stays close. That is the law of large numbers.

Counts do not even out

The second chart shows that the number of extra heads does not shrink back to 0. It grows roughly like √n, but n grows faster, so the proportion still settles.

The coin has no memory

After a run of tails, heads is not "due". Early streaks are not corrected. They are swamped by the many flips that follow.
