A ball is a sum of coin flips

At each of the n rows the ball flips a coin: right counts 1, left counts 0. Its bin number is the total, so it follows a Binomial(n, p) distribution.

That's the CLT at work

A sum of many independent flips is approximately normal, with mean np and SD √(np(1−p)). More rows means more flips per ball, and a closer match to the curve.
