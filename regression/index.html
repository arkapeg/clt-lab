Why "least squares"

Turn on squared residuals and drag a point. The line shifts so the total area of the squares is as small as it can be. It always passes through the point of means (x̄, ȳ).

Outliers pull the line

Load "One outlier" and drag the lone point at the far right. A point with an extreme x value has a lot of leverage over the slope.

r measures straight-line patterns only

Load "Curved". There is a strong relationship, yet r is close to 0, because the pattern is not a straight line. Always look at the plot.
